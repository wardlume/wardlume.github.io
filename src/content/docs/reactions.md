When someone touches a warded Mac, Wardlume can spring a reaction: an image that flashes for a moment, optionally with a sound. Configure it in **Settings → Intruder reactions**.

## Reaction packs

| Pack | Style |
|---|---|
| **Silent Professional** (default) | Office-friendly: a red frame and **ACCESS DENIED**, no sound |
| **Wizard** | A glowering wizard, with sound |
| **Grumpy Old Man** | A stern stare, with sound |

- **Play sound on intrusion:** plays the pack's audio if it has one.
- **Cooldown:** the minimum time between reactions (1, 3, 5, or 10 seconds).
- **Test reaction:** preview it without casting a ward.

## Custom assets

Make the ward your own. Drag a file onto a slot or click **Browse**; leave a slot empty to use the pack's default.

| Slot | What it does | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| **Cover image** | Shows the whole time the ward is on, *in place of* the glass shield | PNG, JPEG, HEIC, GIF · up to 10 MB |
| **Reaction image** | Flashes when someone touches your keyboard or mouse | PNG, JPEG, HEIC, GIF · up to 10 MB |
| **Reaction sound** | Plays with the reaction image | MP3, M4A, WAV · up to 10 MB |

Wardlume copies your files into its own sandboxed folder; your originals are never modified or uploaded. Removing a custom asset deletes only Wardlume's copy.

Want a photo of whoever tries to unlock? See [Intruder photo](/docs/intruder-photo) (optional, off by default).
