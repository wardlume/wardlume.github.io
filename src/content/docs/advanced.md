**Settings → Advanced** holds the rarely needed options.

## Emergency exit

An optional shortcut that **instantly drops the ward with no authentication**. It's **off by default**, and the default combo is **⌘⇧W** (you can change it).

Anyone at the keyboard can use it, so leave it off unless you want a guaranteed way out. You already have several safe ways out without it: Touch ID, Apple Watch, your password, and **⌘⌥Esc → Force Quit Wardlume**. See the [Safety guide](/safety).

## Reset all settings

**Reset all** returns shortcuts, reactions, automation, displays, and gestures to their defaults. Your custom images and sound are kept.
