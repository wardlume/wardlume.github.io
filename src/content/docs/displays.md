Settings live in **Settings → Displays & gestures**.

## Multiple monitors

The ward appears on the **display you activate it from** (the one under your cursor). **Input is locked on every monitor either way.**

For the other monitors, choose:

- **Show as-is** (default): they stay visible, so you can keep an eye on logs or a second agent.
- **Black out**: they go dark while warded.

## Plugging and unplugging

**Unplugging a monitor never unlocks your Mac.** If the warded display disappears, the ward moves to a remaining display, usually the built-in screen, and input stays locked. Plugging a monitor in is handled the same way.

## Trackpad gestures

**Block system gestures** (on by default) suppresses Mission Control, Spaces, and Launchpad swipes while warded. With it off, gestures can switch Spaces over the ward.

> Only the **current Space** is shown behind the glass. Other Spaces can't be displayed by any app.
